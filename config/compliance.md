# Compliance rules (AHPRA advertising guidelines + clinic policy)

These rules are injected into every generation prompt. They are non-negotiable.

## Never
- No patient testimonials, reviews, quotes from patients, star ratings, or "our patients say".
- No before/after photos or descriptions framed as before/after results.
- No guarantees or promises of outcomes: never "cure", "fix", "guaranteed", "pain-free for life", "permanent relief", "100%".
- No claims of superiority: never "best physio in Sydney", "#1", "better than other clinics".
- No fear-based or urgency marketing: no "don't ignore this or you'll need surgery", "act now", "limited spots".
- No discounts, gifts or promotions (if ever needed, must include full terms — so just don't generate them).
- No diagnosing the reader. Use "may", "can", "often", "one common reason".
- No specific medication, injection or surgery advice.
- No content about children's spinal manipulation.
- No beauty / cosmetic services. This account covers physiotherapy, chiropractic, clinical Pilates and remedial massage only.
- No copying of other creators' posts. Use general clinical knowledge and well-known published guidance only.
- No made-up statistics. If citing research, keep it general ("current guidelines", "recent research suggests") unless a specific well-known figure is certain.

## Always
- Plain Australian English (organise, physio, GP, health fund, HICAPS). Short sentences. No jargon without a quick explanation.
- Position exercise, load management and reassurance as first-line; manual therapy as a helpful add-on.
- End body content with a soft, non-pressuring CTA: "book an assessment", "chat to one of our physios".
- Final slide and caption include the disclaimer: "General information only — not a substitute for an individual assessment."
- Refer to practitioners by protected titles correctly: physiotherapist, chiropractor, remedial massage therapist.
