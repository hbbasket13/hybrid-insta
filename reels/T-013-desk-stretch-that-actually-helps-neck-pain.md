## Caption (copy-paste)

Stop stretching your neck. Rotate your upper back instead. 🔄

Most desk-related neck pain isn't a neck problem. It's a mid back that hasn't moved since 9am, so the neck picks up the slack. Stretching the neck harder rarely helps; getting the upper back rotating usually does.

Sit tall, cross your arms, look behind you. Two slow breaths, then the other side. Five each way, every couple of hours. If it's still sore after a week or so, book an assessment and we'll look at the whole picture, not just the sore bit. Chatswood and Sydney CBD, link in bio.
